### Why I focus on IBS...

<div class="pdf-card tutela-card">

**Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)** is a functional gastrointestinal disorder characterized by recurring abdominal discomfort **without** clear structural causes. Beyond physical symptoms including abdominal pain, bloating, diarrhea, and constipation, IBS is strongly linked to **psychological stress and anxiety**.

### IBS in Adolescents

<div class="pdf-card tutela-card">

IBS disproportionately affects adolescents, especially under **academic stress**. In China, **39.4%** of adolescents report IBS symptoms, with prevalence rising sharply before exams, far higher than the overall national rate of **6.5%**.

For teenagers, IBS is not only a medical condition, but **a social and emotional burden**.

</div>
