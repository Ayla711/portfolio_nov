### Why Pig Butchering Scam?

<div class="pdf-row phagex-why" style="--split: minmax(0, 1fr) 26rem">
<div class="pdf-col">

<ul class="phagex-reasons">
  <li>Account for 21% of cases</li>
  <li>Cause the highest losses (avg ¥118,000 vs ¥52,800)</li>
  <li>Backed by criminal networks</li>
  <li>Emotionally manipulative</li>
  <li>Easy to fall into</li>
</ul>

## Designing PhageX

<div class="pdf-headline">

### Future AI Scams

<div class="phagex-warning">

It is increasingly difficult to detect scams
independently...

</div>

</div>

<ul class="pdf-grid phagex-ai">
  <li>

**LLMs** can generate human-like text messages that are even more convincing, grammatically correct, and personalized.

  </li>
  <li>

**Image Generation** tools can create realistic fake images or replace faces in existing photos.

  </li>
  <li>

Scammers may use **voice cloning** to mimic the voice of a boss, relative, or friend to trick victims into taking action.

  </li>
  <li>

**Deepfakes** are hyper-realistic videos digitally manipulated to depict people saying and doing things that never actually happened.

  </li>
</ul>

<div class="pdf-row" style="--split: minmax(0, 1fr) 18rem">
<div class="pdf-col">

<div class="pdf-points">

2. **The Solution:** PhageX (Proactive Defense)<br />A dual-platform system (Glasses + Mobile) leveraging multimodal AI to <span class="pdf-key">scan chats, voices and faces for scam cues</span>, shifting users from vulnerable targets to protected observers.
