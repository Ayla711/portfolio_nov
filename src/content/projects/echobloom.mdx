    </dd>
  </div>

    <dt>Idea</dt>
    <dd>

      music and motion.
    </dd>

      into clips and visuals.
    </dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Outcome</dt>
    <dd>
      An app concept with a three-screen flow, plus a live browser demo shown at

<section id="approach" class="pdf-chapter">

## Approach

### 💡 How to _identify_ and _transform_ moments of frustration?

<div class="pdf-card pdf-card--callout">

I leveraged **Teachable Machine** and the **UrbanSound8K** dataset to
identify noise patterns. The system then transforms these frustration
points into fresh music and visuals.

</div>

#### User Scenario

## Live Demo

<div class="pdf-lead">

Alongside the app concept, I built a browser version of the loop with
p5.js and ml5.js so people could try it in person. It listens through the
microphone, classifies the sound with a Teachable Machine model, and
answers with a music clip and an animation for one of four moods.

</div>
